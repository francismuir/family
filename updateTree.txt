===============================================================================
HOW TO UPDATE THE MUIR FAMILY TREE WEBSITE & DATA EXPORTS
===============================================================================

Your Master Database is the Excel spreadsheet:
    exports/muir_family_tree.xlsx

Whenever you edit that Excel file and run "Update Website.command", it
automatically updates:
  - The entire website in docs/ (all 696+ person pages, 3-generation trees,
    indexes, and the Interactive Tree Explorer)
  - The Ancestry.co.uk GEDCOM file (exports/muir_family_tree.ged)
  - All CSV, XML, and JSON exports in exports/ and docs/exports/


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: OPEN THE MASTER EXCEL SPREADSHEET
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open the folder:
   Documents/Data/Projects/Family Tree/exports/
2. Open "muir_family_tree.xlsx" in Microsoft Excel.
3. Make sure you are on the first worksheet tab: "People".
   (Note: There is also a "How to Edit" tab inside the spreadsheet for quick
   reference.)


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2A: HOW TO EDIT AN EXISTING PERSON
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. On the "People" tab, find the row for the person you want to update
   (you can use Cmd + F in Excel to search by name or Person_ID).
2. Edit any of the following columns directly:
   - Surname          (e.g. MUIR)
   - Given_Names      (e.g. Richard Francis)
   - Birth            (e.g. 1930 July 25th)
   - Death            - Enter a year/date if known (e.g. 2019) -> shows 1930-2019
                      - Leave BLANK if the person is alive     -> shows 1961-
                      - Enter ???? if deceased, year unknown   -> shows 1737-????
   - Marriage         (e.g. 1958 June 14th)
   - Profession       (e.g. Home Civil Service)
   - Notes            (any biographical notes or details)

   TIP ON DATES:
   "Life_Dates" (and all tree boxes) are automatically generated from the
   "Birth" and "Death" columns whenever you rebuild:
     * Blank Death year  = assumed alive (e.g. 1961-)
     * ???? in Death     = deceased, death year unknown (e.g. 1737-????)
     * Year in Death     = deceased with known year (e.g. 1930-2019)


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2B: HOW TO ADD A NEW CHILD
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Scroll to the first blank row at the bottom of the "People" tab.
2. Fill in these columns for the new child:
   - Surname          (e.g. MUIR)
   - Given_Names      (e.g. Thomas Edward)
   - Direct_Ancestor  Enter "Yes" or "No" (usually "No" for new descendants)
   - Gender           Enter "M" or "F"
   - Birth            (e.g. 2024 May 12, London)
   - Death            (leave blank if living)
   - Profession       (optional)
   - Notes            (optional)
   - Parent_IDs       Enter the Person_ID(s) of the parent(s) from Column A,
                      separated by a semicolon (;).
                      Example:  00000242; 00000333
                      (Tip: You can find the parents' Person_IDs by searching
                      for the parents' rows on the "People" tab.)

   WHAT YOU CAN LEAVE BLANK:
   You can leave "Person_ID", "Full_Name", "Generation", "Life_Dates",
   "Parent_Names", and the parents' "Child_IDs" blank.
   When you run the update script, it will automatically:
     - Assign a new unique Person_ID (e.g. P1501)
     - Calculate their Generation number (one generation below their parents)
     - Link them to both parents and all siblings
     - Create their own profile page (docs/people/P1501.html)
     - Add their box and connecting lines onto their parents' 3-generation
       family tree chart.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2C: HOW TO ADD A NEW SPOUSE OR PARTNER
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Scroll to the first blank row at the bottom of the "People" tab.
2. Fill in these columns for the new spouse/partner:
   - Surname          (their surname / maiden name)
   - Given_Names      (their given names)
   - Direct_Ancestor  "No" (or "Yes" if a direct ancestor)
   - Gender           "M" or "F"
   - Birth            (optional)
   - Death            (optional)
   - Marriage         (e.g. 2023 August 10th)
   - Profession       (optional)
   - Notes            (optional)
   - Spouse_IDs       Enter their partner's Person_ID from Column A
                      (for example: 00000567 or 00000568).

   WHAT YOU CAN LEAVE BLANK:
   Leave "Person_ID", "Generation", and "Life_Dates" blank — the script will
   fill them in automatically and link both partners to each other.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: SAVE EXCEL & REBUILD THE WEBSITE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Save your changes in Excel (Cmd + S).
2. Open your main project folder in Finder:
   Documents/Data/Projects/Family Tree/
3. Double-click:
   Update Website.command
4. A Terminal window will open and rebuild all website pages and GEDCOM/data
   exports in about 2 seconds.
5. At the end, it will ask:
   "Would you also like to commit and publish these updates to
    https://francismuir.github.io/family/ now? (y/n)"
   - Press "y" to automatically commit and push your updated website to GitHub
     Pages (it goes live on https://francismuir.github.io/family/ in ~1 minute).
   - Or press "n" if you only want to view the changes locally on your Mac first
     (open docs/index.html in your browser to preview).
