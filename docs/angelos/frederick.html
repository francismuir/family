FREDERICK JOSEPH JOHN ANGELO
1800-1869 (No 44) (P)

Parents Anthony Angelo Malevolti Tremamondo (No 88)
1747-1829
Elizabeth Martha Bland (No 89)
Born London, January 26th 1800
Military Career (E.I.C.)
1819 Cadet
Jun 14th 1820 Admitted
Jan 10th 1821 Cornet
Aug 14th 1822 Lieutenant
Aug 26th 1831 Captain
Jul 26th 1841 Major
Sep 28th 1841 Invalided
Service :- Z/L/MIL/10/1
Jun 28th 1820 Directed to join GGBG Red 133
1821 Posted to 7th Light Cavalry Black 5.21
Jul 2nd 1825 Adjutant 7th LC (till May 29th 1826)
Jan 6th 1832 DJAG Dinapore & Benares Divs.
Dec 29th 1835 ditto Sirhind Div. (till Dec 30th 1840)
1838-9 JAG to Army of the Indus at Ferezepore during first Afghan War
(Was living in Calcutta in 1857)
Marriage Dec 15th 1825 to Catherine Ogden Anderson 1802-1863 (No 45)
Children (1)Frederick Cortlandt 1826-1857 (No 22)
(2)Elliot
(3)John (m. Emily Blyth)
(4)Catherine Elizabeth (m. Capt. Short)
(5)Maria Sawyer (m. Sir Richard Oldfield)
(6)Martin Kenneth (m. Mary Farrel) 1837-19??
(7)Lucy Caroline (m. Harry Anderson)
(8)Louisa Caroline (m. Capt. R Evans)
Died Jan 22nd 1869 at Ootacumund (abscess of the liver)
Buried Day of death by C P Dury (Acting Chaplain) Burial record shows rank as "Colonel, Bengal Army"
Memorials None extant at Ootacumund. It is doubtful if the grave was ever permanently marked. His wife has a marked grave in St Stephen's churchyard
See also (1)"Historical Records of the Viceroy's Bodyguard" by Lt V C P Hodson (1910) p228
(2)The Times Mar 16th 1869 (notice of death)

RFM Angelo44 revised 12/98
