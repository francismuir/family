Work in progress

I've had many comments and much useful advice from various people. Here are some of the things that I intend to incorporate in to this site when I get a chance (when my father stops sending me up new family trees to work on - so it might never happen!). Thank you to all those people who sent me e-mails.

  • Include information on where people are located as part of the family tree. Thanks to Alan Paterson for that.
  • Include a list of all names on the family trees in alphabetical order. Thanks to Barbara Nellesen from Georgia, USA for this suggestion. Also to Paul Hume who has such a list on his excellent web site. UPDATE - Added this on 24th August, 2000.
  • Once I have done this I will transfer all the notes on to the individual people's pages (once I have cross referenced them to the family trees).
  • Include an overview of the history of our family - I'm waiting for my father to write this.
