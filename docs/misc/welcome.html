Welcome to the Muir's family tree

Welcome to the Muir's family tree. This project was started as a 70th birthday present for my father, Richard Muir, whose birthday was on the 25th July, 2000. All the material on this site was researched by him over a number of years. The idea for this site came from my wife Lindsey Muir.

My intention is to include all of my father's material on this site and, one day, to add some material of my own. It's a huge task and this site will be permanently under construction. I hope you find something useful here.
