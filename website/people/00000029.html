ANGELO**, Frederick Cortlandt (1826-1857)

Surname ANGELO**
CName Frederick Cortlandt
Generation 4
Birth 1826 Oct 6th, Kurnaul India
Profession Bengal Army, mainly 16th BNI
Death 1857 Jun 27th, in the massacre at Cawnpore.
Notes Born at Karnal 6 Oct 1826. Educated by Dr Grieg of Leytonstone and at Addiscombe (1843-4). Returned to India "overland" 1845. Ensign 16th Bengal Native Infantry 10 Feb 1846, Lieutenant 6 Jan 1850. Faced General Court Martial at Dinapore 25 Oct 1847 for "unbecoming conduct", sentenced to "severe reprimand". Killed in the massacre at Cawnpore 27 June 1857. Married in Benares 31 Oct 1850 Memorial in All Saints Church Cawnpore.
Spouse Helena Elizabeth Gordon CUMMING (1817-1908)
Marriage 1850 Oct 31st, Benares India
