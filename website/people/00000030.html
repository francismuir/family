ANGELO**, Frederick John Joseph (1800-1869)

Surname ANGELO**
CName Frederick John Joseph
Generation 5
Birth 1800 Jan 26th, London
Profession Bengal Cavalry, (7th LC)
Death 1869 Jan 22nd, Ootacamund
Notes Born London 26 Jan 1800. Cadet 1819. Admitted 14 June 1820. Cornet 10 Jan 1820. Lieutenant 14 Aug 1822. Captain 26 Aug 1831. Major 26 July 1841. Invalided 28 Sept 1841. Died Ootacamund 22 Jan 1869. Service: joined GG's Bodyguard 1820, posted as Cornet to 7th Light Cavalry 1821, Adjutant 7th LC 1825-26, Deputy Judge Advocate General Dinapore & Benares divisions 1832-1840 (also Sirhind Division 1835). Judge Advocate General to the Army of the Indus at Ferozapore during 1st Afghan War 1838-39. Married 15 December 1825.
